## 10. Questions before you choose (home.json: faq)

**H2:** Questions before you choose

**What is managed WordPress hosting?**
Managed WordPress hosting means the host looks after the server your site runs on, so you can focus on your content and your customers. DronaHost offers it on its WordPress hosting plans. If you want to know what is included before you buy, ask the team by support ticket or email.

`[HUMAN REVIEW: add the exact tasks once managed-hosting-tasks is verified.]`

**Do I need technical skills to use DronaHost?**
Not necessarily. You can contact the team by support ticket or email at any hour, and we reply within 5 minutes. We move your existing site for free, and your site gets automatic backups. If a task is unclear, ask the team before you buy.

**Does DronaHost host email?**
No. Email is not hosted on DronaHost servers, and Business Hosting does not include email. Your hosting and your email are separate, so you will need an email service from another provider. If you are unsure which option fits, write to the team before you buy.

**Can I move my existing WordPress site to DronaHost?**
Yes, you can move your existing WordPress site to DronaHost. Moving is free on every package. Each hosting package includes the move of one website, and a VPS includes up to 10 websites. The migration terms page explains what is covered. To start, open the free migration page and the team will reply.

**Will the price go up when my plan renews?**
No. The hosting price does not change on renewal, because the renewal price equals the signup price. Each plan shows its renewal price next to its signup price. The pricing policy has the full terms. Prices exclude tax. Customers in India pay GST at checkout.

**What if I am not happy with my plan?**
DronaHost offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. The refund policy sets out the exact terms, so please read it before you buy. If you have a question about the guarantee, contact the team by support ticket or email and we will reply within 5 minutes.

**How do I reach support?**
You can reach the team by support ticket or email. Support is available 24/7, with a reply within 5 minutes. Tickets and email are the only support channels we offer. The support policy explains what 24/7 and the reply time mean, so read it for the details.
