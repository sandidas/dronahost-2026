# Crumb and Co: voice and terms (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

## Voice

Warm, plain and specific. Short sentences. Say what the bread is and when it arrives. No food-marketing clichés.

## Words we use

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| bread box | goodie bag |
| loaf | artisan-crafted loaf |
| order | pre-order |

## Words we avoid

See `bannedPhrases` in `voice.json` (`npm run content:voice` prints the whole voice).

## Channels

We take orders on the website only. Orders by phone and by text message are not offered, so copy never mentions them.
