# Interview: /cloud-web-hosting (cloud hosting for small business)

Answers are recorded word for word with the date. An unanswered question is `GAP: <question>`.

```
Q0 Every product page shows the same four plans (decision 73). What makes the cloud hosting offer "cloud"?
A0 "Same plans, cloud infrastructure": same four plans; "cloud" means they run on major cloud providers' infrastructure and you can move up a plan as you grow. The page leads with that, honestly.  (2026-10-24, chosen option)
Artifact: not given

Q1 Who buys cloud hosting from DronaHost (rather than WordPress or Business hosting), and why?
A1 "Growing small businesses": small businesses whose traffic is rising or uneven, who want room to grow without changing host.  (2026-10-24, chosen option)
Artifact: not given

Q2 What do small-business buyers worry about first when they look at cloud hosting?
A2 "Too technical to manage, Unpredictable bills, Site slow or down at peaks, Moving without breaking,  Trust and relatibility and support"  (2026-10-24, chosen options plus own words)
Artifact: not given

Q3 What is the honest limit of DronaHost cloud hosting? The page has to say it.
A3 "Email not included"  (2026-10-24, chosen option)
Artifact: not given

Q3 follow-up: Readers expect "cloud" to mean automatic scaling. When a site outgrows its plan, what actually happens?
A3b "They move up a plan": fixed plans; no automatic scaling. The customer (or the team) moves them to a larger plan. Page says so plainly.  (2026-10-24, chosen option)
Artifact: not given

Q4 What do cloud and hosting customers ask support about most?
A4 "Which plan to choose"  (2026-10-24, chosen option)
Artifact: not given
```
