## Notes for the draft (not owner words)

- A1 names needs, not buyers. "Seamless", "robust" and "blazing fast" are banned phrases (voice.json); they stay verbatim here and never reach the page.
- A1 adds a new claim, "Premium theme and plugins support": not in claims.ts or pricing.json. It needs a definition and a claim before the page can state it.

- The follow-up answer repeats the examples Claude offered in the question, and adds "who need real wordpress expert support". The owner confirms at read-back that each one describes real customers. A2 also matches Claude's examples; same check. From Q3 on, Claude asks without examples.

- A3 is an acceptable-use rule. Acceptable use is a Tier B legal document (decision 24), so the page states it in one plain line and links the acceptable use policy only once it is reviewed and published. Product limits already on file (no email, no live chat or phone, no staging on Starter if F9 is confirmed, one backup copy kept if F10 is confirmed) join it in HonestLimits.

- A4: "customers rarely need support" is a first-hand observation, not a measured fact. The page may say what we do (we look after the hosting so problems are caught on our side) only where a claim backs it; it does not claim a low ticket count without numbers. Discount requests are answered by the pricing model: the same price at renewal (F13) and annual billing, with no coupon promised.
